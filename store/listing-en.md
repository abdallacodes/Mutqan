## Full description (4000 characters max)
Mutqan helps you revise and secure what you have memorised of the Quran, with a plan that fits each day.

Start from what you know: tell the app which surahs or juz you have memorised. Your plan is built only from them, so you are never asked to revise what you have not learned.

A plan for every day
• Today shows what to revise, and how much.
• When you finish, tap "I've finished revising" and say how it went. Weak pages come back more often.
• Choose a fixed amount a day, or let the app put the weakest first.

The Mushaf, page by page
• The Madinah Mushaf in the King Fahd Complex fonts, one page or a two-page spread.
• Tap any verse: save it to your passages, mark it weak, or see the verses that look like it.
• Long-press a verse to start a subject there, so what you know is arranged by topic.

Memory map
• A map of the juz and hizb quarters, coloured by how strong your memory is, shows where to look first.
• Suggestions for groups of similar verses that you tend to confuse.

Practice
• Test yourself on pages and verses, and log how each revision went.
• Keep similar verses in groups that you revise together.

Recitations
• Listen to several reciters, with controls in the notification while the screen is off.
• Download recitations to listen without internet.

Your data stays yours
• Works without an account; your data stays on your device.
• Back up and restore with a file, with an optional password.
• Optional sync between your devices with a Google account, and you can delete the account inside the app.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Arabic first, with English too, and several colour themes that are easy on the eyes.
